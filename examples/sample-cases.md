# Cold War Rescue - Usage Examples & Test Cases (使用示例与测试用例)

This document contains test cases demonstrating how the `cold-war-rescue` skill analyzes different conflict scenarios and generates appropriate responses.

---

## Case 1: Weekend Trip Disagreement (Weekend Trip Standoff)

### User Input:
> "我们吵架冷战两天了。原因是周末想去哪里玩意见不一样，他说我每次都自作主张不听他的意见，最后他摔门进房间就再没和我说话。我现在觉得挺委屈的，但我也不想一直耗着，怎么发消息开口？"

### Agent Output:

🩹 局面诊断

对方现在大概率是**防御与被忽视感叠加型**，核心诉求可能是“**在关系决策中希望被尊重和被听取**”。
而你这边，真正在意的其实是“**计划被否定以及对方摔门冷处理带来的委屈感**”。
这件事本质上是**感受分歧与沟通模式冲突**，冷战 2 天属于**情绪阶段（气头/等待台阶期）**。

💡 破冰策略

先针对“没照顾到对方感受”轻微给个台阶，降低防御，避开争论“谁对谁错”。

📱 话术（选一条直接发）

【体面版】
"这两天想想，上次定路线时我确实太急了，没认真听完你的想法。我们各自冷静了两天，晚点抽空聊聊你的方案？"

【柔软版】
"别生气啦，这两天心里空落落的。那天是我太固执没顾及你的想法，晚饭想吃什么？我买回去咱们边吃边聊。"

【洒脱版】
"门摔得挺响，这两天手还疼不？不气的话出来吃个饭，这次行程听你的，你说了算。"

⚠️ 别踩的坑

- 严禁发“至于为了个去处发这么大火吗”——这属于二次指责，会瞬间点燃怒火。
- 不要急着在第一句话里要求对方为你“摔门”的行为道歉，先建立沟通通路。

## Case 2: Silent Treatment After Forgotten Anniversary (忘记纪念日后的冷战)

### User Input:
> "I forgot our 1-year anniversary yesterday because of a work deadline. She didn't yell, but she gave me the cold shoulder and has been ignoring my texts since last night. How do I fix this without sounding like I'm making excuses?"

### Agent Output:

🩹 Diagnosis

Partner State: Likely in **Deep Hurt & Disappointment Phase**; Core need is "To feel cherished and valued over work".
User State: Core focus is "Guilt over oversight, but worried about sounding dismissive with work excuses".
Conflict Essence: Emotional Disagreement; Cold War (1 Day) is in the **High Emotional Tension Phase**.

💡 Icebreaker Strategy

Acknowledge the emotional impact immediately without hiding behind work deadlines, accompanied by a concrete action to remedy.

📱 Messages (Pick one to send)

[Dignified Version]
"I know I dropped the ball completely yesterday, and you have every right to be upset. Work isn't an excuse for forgetting what matters most to us—can I take you out tonight to properly celebrate?"

[Soft Version]
"I feel terrible about yesterday. Missing our anniversary broke my heart too, and I hate seeing you hurt. Please let me make it up to you tonight."

[Casual Version]
"I messed up big time yesterday. Penalty accepted—I'm ready to take my punishment and make it up to you with your favorite dinner tonight?"

⚠️ Pitfalls to Avoid

- Do NOT lead with "I was so busy with work" as the primary reason—it validates her fear that work comes before her.
- Avoid asking "Are you still mad?"—focus instead on owning the mistake and proposing a fix.
